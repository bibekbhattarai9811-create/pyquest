Every decision a program makes comes down to a yes or a no. In Python, that's True or False, with a capital letter and no quotes. This module builds that foundation. You'll compare two values with the double equals sign, use greater-than and less-than, and then combine questions with and, or, and not. For example: is the visitor old enough, and do they have a ticket. Get comfortable here, because the next module, making choices with if, depends entirely on it. Let's look at True and False.

Often you don't have one value, you have many: a list of scores, a list of names, a shopping list. Python's list keeps them in order, inside square brackets. In this module you'll build a list, pick out an item by its position, counting from zero, grab the last item with minus one, count how many there are, add to the end, change an item, and check whether something is in it. Lists show up constantly, especially with loops in the next module. Let's make one.

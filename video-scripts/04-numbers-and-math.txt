Python is a very capable calculator. In this module you'll work through the maths operators one at a time: adding and subtracting, multiplying and dividing, and three that are new to most people. Floor division, written with two slashes, throws away the remainder. The percent sign keeps only the remainder. And two stars means to the power of. You'll also see why brackets change the answer, and how to round a decimal to a couple of places. Each lesson is a single fix: change one operator, add one pair of brackets. Let's add some numbers.
